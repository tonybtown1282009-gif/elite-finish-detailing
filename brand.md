# Tony's Detailing — Brand & Service Guide

Professional mobile auto detailing based in **Chardon, Ohio**, serving **Geauga County** and **Cuyahoga County**.

---

## 1. Brand Identity

**Personality:** Clean. Professional. Confident. Local.

Tony's Detailing is a mobile-first service brand built on craft, reliability, and an honest finish. The brand voice should sound like a detailer who knows the work — direct, no fluff, and never overpromises. Visually, the brand should feel like a freshly finished black sedan parked under a clean light: deep, glossy, and quietly premium.

**Tone guidelines:**
- Direct and factual — describe the work, not the hype.
- Confident, never boastful. Avoid superlatives like "best" or "perfect."
- Local-first language: name the towns, name the counties, sound like a neighbor.
- Set expectations up front, especially around pricing, distance, and condition.

---

## 2. Color Palette

The palette is intentionally tight — four colors, used with discipline.

| Role         | Color        | Hex       | Usage |
|--------------|--------------|-----------|-------|
| Primary      | White        | `#ffffff` | Primary background, page and card surfaces |
| Accent       | Steel Blue   | `#1e3a5f` | Headings, buttons, logo accent, link color |
| Text         | Deep Black   | `#0a0a0a` | Primary body text, wordmark on light surfaces |
| Neutral      | Light Grey   | `#d9d9d9` | Dividers, card borders, subtle section backgrounds |

**Rules:**
- Default to **white-dominant layouts** with steel blue accents and deep black text.
- Steel blue is the only accent — use it for headings, buttons, and the logo mark.
- Light grey is structural only — dividers, borders, and subtle fills. Never use it for text on white (contrast is too low to read).
- Do not introduce new accent colors (no reds, no chrome silvers, no metallics).

---

## 3. Typography

**Family:** Bold, modern sans-serif. No scripts, no serifs, no handwritten faces.

Recommended pairings (any one of these is acceptable):
- **Inter** (700 for headings, 400/500 for body)
- **Montserrat** (700 / 400)
- **Manrope** (700 / 500)
- **Helvetica Neue** / **Arial** as system fallbacks

**Hierarchy:**
- **H1 (page title):** Sans-serif, 700 weight, uppercase or sentence case, generous tracking.
- **H2 (section):** Sans-serif, 700, sentence case.
- **H3 (service name):** Sans-serif, 600, slightly tighter tracking.
- **Body:** Sans-serif, 400/500, comfortable line height (1.5+).
- **Captions / fine print:** Sans-serif, 400 weight, deep black. De-emphasize through weight and size, not color — light grey is not legible as text on a white background.

**Do not:**
- Mix more than two type weights in a single layout block.
- Use italic for emphasis — use weight instead.
- Use any script, decorative, or display face.

---

## 4. Logo Concept

- **Background:** White field.
- **Mark:** Steel blue with optional deep black detail — a subtle gloss highlight or a clean side-profile car silhouette. The mark should read at a glance on a 1-inch business card and on a truck door.
- **Wordmark:** "TONY'S DETAILING" set in the brand sans-serif, 700 weight, deep black or steel blue depending on placement.
- **Negative space matters.** Keep clear space around the mark equal to the cap-height of the wordmark.
- **One-color variants:** Provide an all-black version (for light surfaces and print) and an all-white knockout version (for use over photography).

---

## 5. Responsive Design Principles

All brand surfaces — site, menu cards, social graphics — must work mobile-first.

- **Mobile:** Single-column stack. Full-width sections. Comfortable tap targets. Service blocks stack vertically with clear dividers.
- **Desktop / tablet:** Card or grid layout. Services arranged 2–3 across with consistent card height. Maintain generous whitespace; never crowd the grid.
- **Imagery:** High-contrast photography. Wet paint, reflections, and clean interiors. Avoid washed-out daylight shots that flatten the steel blue.
- **Buttons:** Steel blue fill, white text, square or 4px-rounded corners. No gradients, no drop shadows.

## 7. Pricing Notes

- **All prices are listed as ranges.** Final pricing is confirmed at booking or on arrival.
- **Vehicle size matters.** Sedans price at the low end of each range. SUVs and trucks price toward the high end. Oversized vehicles (3-row SUVs, full-size pickups, vans) may exceed the listed range.
- **Location matters.** A **distance upcharge** applies for service locations beyond approximately **15 miles from Chardon, Ohio**. The upcharge scales with distance and is quoted before the appointment is confirmed.
- **Outside the local area:** Only the **Full Detail** package (or higher, once available) is bookable. See Section 6.3.

## 8. Condition Upcharges

Some conditions require extra time, extra product, or extra equipment. These upcharges are **assessed on arrival, after a quick walk-around inspection** — they are never quoted blindly over the phone or by text.

| Condition                 | Typical Upcharge |
|---------------------------|------------------|
| Pet hair                  | $25 – $75        |
| Deep stains               | $20 – $60        |
| Smoke / persistent odor   | $50 – $100       |
| Bio hazard                | $100+ (case-by-case) |
| Excessive dirt or debris  | $20 – $50        |

**How it works:**
1. We arrive, walk the vehicle with you, and identify any qualifying conditions.
2. We quote the upcharge before any work begins.
3. You approve before we start. No surprises on the invoice.

## 10. Limitations & Conditions *(draft — full policy forthcoming)*

> **This section is conditional / draft.** A complete service policy and client agreement is in development and will replace this notice once finalized.

Detailing can do a lot — but not everything. Some conditions are tied to the age, history, or material wear of a vehicle and may not be fully resolvable through detailing alone, including:

- **Permanent stains** that have set into fabric, carpet, or headliner over time.
- **Salt damage** to carpets, mats, and lower exterior panels common to Northeast Ohio winters.
- **Rust** and other oxidation of metal surfaces.
- **Deeply set odors** from long-term smoke, pets, or moisture intrusion.
- **Pre-existing paint defects** such as deep scratches, swirl marks beyond a wash's reach, clear coat failure, or oxidation.

**Results are not guaranteed for pre-existing damage.** We will always communicate what is and isn't achievable before starting work, and we will never charge for a result we don't believe we can deliver.
